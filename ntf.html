Questions

Before you register

Who can join?

The fest is built for young people in Nsukka. Students, graduates, job seekers and young business owners are all welcome, from complete beginners to people who already work in tech.

Can I join more than one track?

You can choose up to three tracks on the form. The organisers confirm your final placement so every session has the right number of people.

Do I need my own laptop?

A laptop helps, but it is not a must. Tell us what you can bring on the form so the team can plan the hub computers.

What does it cost?

Ask the organisers for the latest on fees and cohorts. Call or message +234 707 551 0773 on WhatsApp.

How do I know my registration went through?

After you create your badge, send your details to the organisers on WhatsApp. Your registration is complete once they receive that message, and you will get your reference there.
